Settings

Tunnel and Wi-Fi

Mullvad is the normal path. Wi-Fi is the network devices join.

Tunnel

Connect and Disconnect start or stop the tunnel. They keep the country already pinned. To change country, use the list on the right.

If the tunnel drops, YumPi brings it back. Disconnect is the one choice that stays off.

Devices set to Mullvad lose the internet until the tunnel is back. Devices set to Direct keep your normal connection. This dashboard and other devices on your network stay reachable.
